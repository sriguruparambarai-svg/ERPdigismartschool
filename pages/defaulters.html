Upload the teachers' Daily Check Sheets
Take a photo of each sheet — flat, the whole page, in good light — and choose it here (you can choose several at once). The app reads the roll numbers marked ✗. Check each list against the photo (tap a number to add or remove it), then press Save. Saved messages go to parents at 4 PM with the homework. The sheets are printed from TeachBot → School Admin → Daily Check Sheet.
Repeat defaulters — did the parent see the warning?
When a child is a defaulter 3 times in a week (homework, class test and discipline counted separately), the parent gets one warning at 4 PM and must tap “I have seen”. Parents who haven't tapped it in 2 days are listed first — please call them and press Called. They also show in the owner's 6 PM report.

The words parents read
Change the message for each kind in your school's own words. The English box goes first, the Tamil box just below it. Leave a box empty to send only the other language (for example, empty the English box for Tamil only). These blanks fill in by themselves: {name} child's name · {date} date · {subject} · {lesson} · {marks} and {total} (low marks) · {items} (discipline: uniform, nails…). New wording is used for children marked after you save; messages already waiting keep their old words.
